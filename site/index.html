GST Report — GSTR-1 / 3B / 2A-2B

These reports are DRAFTS — verify by uploading on the portal. The app does not file; enter ITC yourself from portal 2B.

ITC (portal 2B se — khud bharein, ₹)

GSTR-2A / 2B Reconciliation

e-Invoice / e-Way Bill JSON (DRAFT)
